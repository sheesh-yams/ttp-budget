SlateSuite on mobile — mockups for review

Proposed mobile layouts for the projects dashboard, the project page and its sub-menu, and the pre-production tools (deal memos, schedule, call sheets). On phones the sidebar goes away: a bottom tab bar handles app-level navigation, and a project carries its own section switcher. The deal memo flow is rebuilt as short one-thumb steps, so a memo can be drafted and sent from set.

No sidebar under 768pxThumb-zone actions at the bottomOne task per screenCards, not tablesGlobal “+” to create anything

1 · Home

Projects dashboard

The sidebar becomes a five-slot bottom bar: Home, Projects, a centre “+”, Money, More. KPIs become a swipeable strip, the project table becomes cards, and anything shooting today is pinned to the top.

2 · Project

Project overview and its sub-menu: two options

The project sub-menu (Sales · Pre-prod · Delivery) needs a mobile home. Option A keeps the app's bottom bar and puts the sections in a swipeable chip row under the project header. Option B swaps the bottom bar for a project-level one while you're inside a project, grouping the sections the way the sidebar does today.

3 · Pre-prod

Deal memos: draft and send on the go

The board becomes a list grouped by budget role, with bids and budget deltas. Drafting is a four-step flow with the main action always in the thumb zone: who → rate → terms → review & send. The internal budget panel stays, clearly marked as never shown to the vendor.

4 · Pre-prod

Schedule and call sheets

On a phone, the schedule is a day picker plus a vertical timeline. Call sheets lead with what crew ask for most: call time, location, weather and “when am I in?”.

Next step

How this would ship

1 · Shell. Below 768px: hide the sidebar, add the bottom tab bar and the “+” sheet, and move the workspace switcher into the top bar. This one change fixes the half-screen sidebar on every page.
2 · Project. Option A or B for the sub-menu, the overview as cards, and pre-prod hubs that show status.
3 · Deal memos. The list by role, plus the four-step draft flow on phones. It reuses the existing actions; the desktop editor stays as it is.
4 · Schedule & call sheets. Day picker plus timeline, and the call sheet read view with a send-update action.
